# Public source notes

Accessed on 2026-06-04. These public pages informed the demonstration taxonomy and FAQ wording. The repository does not copy private contact logs or patient records.

1. Pharmacy2U home page: https://www.pharmacy2u.co.uk/
   - Public service lines include NHS prescription ordering, medicine stock checking, and order tracking.
2. Pharmacy2U contact page: https://www.pharmacy2u.co.uk/help-and-support/articles/contacting-pharmacy2u/click-here-to-contact-pharmacy2u/69cbbfad4c25c664abb592ed
   - Public contact routing distinguishes NHS Prescriptions, Private Online Doctor, Pet Health, and Shop Orders / over-the-counter items.
3. Pharmacy2U prescription tracking page: https://www.pharmacy2u.co.uk/prescriptions/track-your-prescription-delivery
   - Public tracking information describes order stages beginning with GP approval and carrier tracking after dispatch.
4. Pharmacy2U NHS prescription FAQ: https://www.pharmacy2u.co.uk/prescriptions/frequently-asked-questions
   - Public FAQ content covers delivery, tracking, and delivery-address updates.
5. Pharmacy2U GP workflow page: https://www.pharmacy2u.co.uk/prescriptions/how-we-work-with-your-gp
   - Public workflow explains that medicine requests are forwarded to a GP surgery for approval or decline.
6. NHS App prescription help: https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-app/help/prescriptions/requesting-a-prescription/
   - Public NHS guidance explains GP approval status and directs urgent medicine requests to appropriate clinical support.
