# CV entry

**Digital Pharmacy Voice-of-the-Customer Intelligence, Taxonomy Evolution and Safe Automation Workbench**  
*Python, SQL, scikit-learn, NLP, text analytics, Streamlit, Plotly*

- Built an end-to-end NLP pipeline and ten-page Streamlit dashboard for 30,000 synthetic multi-channel digital-pharmacy contacts, covering text normalisation, personal-data redaction, intent classification, sentiment analysis, emerging-issue monitoring, CRM linkage, and safe routing.
- Designed a 22-intent pharmacy-service taxonomy and a human-review workflow that clusters out-of-scope contacts, surfaces representative examples, records taxonomy versions, and identifies candidate intents for operational review.
- Benchmarked lexical and local dense-embedding models using random and temporal holdouts, channel-level metrics, confidence calibration, and coverage–accuracy analysis; achieved 0.933 Macro-F1 on the held-out synthetic intent test set and routed 100% of generated urgent cases to clinical escalation.
- Added an interpretable synthetic CRM driver analysis for low satisfaction and retention outcomes, together with an FAQ retrieval benchmark and optional connected-environment scripts for Sentence Transformers and Hugging Face transformer experiments.
